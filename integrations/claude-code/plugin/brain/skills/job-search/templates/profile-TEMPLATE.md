# Job search profile

Copy this file to `80-Private/job-search/profile.md` in your vault and fill it in. `80-Private/` is
local and never pushed. The job-search skill reads it to judge fit; it never sends it anywhere.

## Target roles

List the role titles you want, most wanted first. Add titles you do NOT want if they are easy to
confuse with these.

- <role title>
- <role title>

## Level and scope

What level and what kind of responsibility you want (individual contributor, lead, manager), and what
you want to avoid.

## Experience

Short entries, most recent first. Only facts you are happy for the ranking to rely on.

- <role>, <organization type or sector>, <years>: <what you did, in one or two lines>

## Skills

- <skill or technology>

## Domains

Sectors where your experience is a real advantage. These raise a posting's score; they do not filter.

- <domain>

## Languages

- <language>: <level>

## What you bring

Three to five lines in your own words. The skill uses this to write the strengths column honestly.
