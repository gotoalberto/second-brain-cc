Every Sunday, prepare a weekly review note.

1. `brain recall "decision" --recent 50` and `brain recent 50` to see the week's activity.
2. Group the week into themes; list open questions and next steps.
3. Save it as `30-Knowledge/$(date +%F)-weekly-review.md` (type: note, tag: review),
   linking related notes with [[wikilinks]] so the graph connects.

Do not invent activity; only summarize what the vault actually contains.
