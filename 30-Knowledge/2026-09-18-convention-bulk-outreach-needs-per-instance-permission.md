## What happened

The user asked to gather a list of people from a shared channel and then contact every one of them
automatically. The collection was done. The mass
send was declined, and so was a first counter offer to send them all "in bulk", which was the same
thing faster.

## The rule

Connection requests, messages, emails or any other contact sent to third parties at scale on a
blanket instruction ("connect with all of them", "message everyone on this list") are not executed,
even when the instruction is explicit and clear. The channel does not matter.

## Why

The action lands on other people, is visible to them and cannot be cleanly undone once sent. They
did not ask to be contacted this way: sharing a link in a group is not consent to be approached one
by one. A blanket instruction from the user is not the same as permission for each action when the
action lands on someone else rather than on the user's own systems.

## How to apply

When a task would need a loop that sends something to people who have not agreed to be contacted,
stop before the loop and offer one of these instead:

- hand over the gathered list and material and let the user act on it;
- go one item at a time, with an explicit confirmation before each send;
- let the user do the sending.

The research half (finding the people, compiling the list) is fine and should be finished. Only the
outward send at scale stops short of a blanket instruction.
