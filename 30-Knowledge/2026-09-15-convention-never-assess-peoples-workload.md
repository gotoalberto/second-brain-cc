## The rule

Never talk about how much work a person carries, in anything: status snapshots, web pages and
artifacts, tickets and comments, vault notes, emails, chat messages and replies. It covers everyone,
not only the user's own team.

## What that excludes

- Words and phrases such as "overloaded", "busy", "stretched", "a lot on their plate", "single point
  of failure", "doing their best but it is not enough", "moderate load", "idle".
- Load labels, load pills, load bars, capacity meters, or counts of items per person presented as a
  measure of load.
- Quoting someone else's remark about a person's load, even verbatim from a meeting.

## What to write instead

The work and the facts: what each item is, its status, what is left, estimates and where they come
from, dates, dependencies and blockers. If ownership is unclear or a deadline was missed, say it as a
fact about the item, never about the person.

## Why

A first team snapshot carried a "load" line per person and load tags on the published page. A
judgment about how much someone carries is not the agent's to make, travels badly when a page is
shared, and says nothing the facts about the work do not already say.
